## 1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW

**Name:** Finomaly

**One-line description:** Context-aware ML and rule-based anomaly detection that flags suspicious GST invoices and filing mismatches before they become penalties.

**Core problem it solves:** MSMEs and their accountants manually review hundreds of GST invoices per month. Errors and fraud slip through: invalid GSTINs, wrong tax math, duplicate invoices, fake or spiking vendor bills, and ITC (Input Tax Credit) mismatches. These cause notices, blocked ITC, and penalties. A fixed amount threshold fails because "normal" differs for every vendor. Finomaly compares each invoice to that vendor's own history and to GST rules, then explains why it was flagged.

**Target users:**
- MSME owners and shopkeepers
- Chartered accountants and GST practitioners
- Internal finance and audit teams

**Principle (show this in the UI):** *An anomaly is not proof of fraud.* Finomaly is decision support. A human makes the final call through a review workflow.

15. **Model Insights page:** model info (Isolation Forest, contamination, features used), a score distribution histogram, and an honest "Limitations & Responsible Use" card stating the model is trained on synthetic data and is not proof of fraud.
16. **Batch CSV upload:** analyze many invoices at once and show a summary toast and results table.
17. **Dark/light theme toggle**, a global command-style search box, and PDF/CSV export of a flagged-invoice report (CSV at minimum).

### 6.3 External APIs
No paid or key-gated API is required, and the app must be fully functional offline. Do not call any GST government portal (they require authentication). Use these local and optional resources only:
- **Local:** Tesseract OCR through pytesseract (optional, with fallback).
- **Optional:** Anthropic Messages API `POST https://api.anthropic.com/v1/messages`, only if the `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` secret exists, to rewrite the explanation text. Wrap it in try/except and fall back to the template.
- **Static data embedded in code:** the GST state-code table (01–38, e.g. 27 Maharashtra, 29 Karnataka, 33 Tamil Nadu, 36 Telangana, 07 Delhi, 24 Gujarat, 09 Uttar Pradesh, 19 West Bengal) and valid GST slabs `[0, 5, 12, 18, 28]`.

### 6.4 Feature engineering (`features.py`)
For each invoice, compute:
`log_total_amount`, `taxable_value`, `item_count`, `effective_tax_ratio` (tax/taxable), `gst_rate`, `vendor_amount_zscore`, `vendor_amount_percentile`, `amount_to_vendor_median_ratio`, `days_since_vendor_last_invoice`, `is_round_amount` (amount % 1000 == 0), `is_weekend`, `invoices_same_day_for_vendor`, `tax_math_error` (abs diff between computed and stated tax).
For a vendor with fewer than 5 invoices, fall back to global statistics.

### 6.5 Detection engine (`detector.py`, `rules.py`)
- Train `IsolationForest(n_estimators=200, contamination=0.08, random_state=42)` on the seeded data, on StandardScaler-scaled features. Save with joblib. Retrain endpoint is optional.
- Convert `decision_function` to a 0–100 anomaly score (min-max normalized against training scores, inverted so higher = riskier).
- **Rule engine**, each rule returns `{code, severity (low/med/high), message, weight}`:
  - `INVALID_GSTIN`: regex `^[0-9]{2}[A-Z]{5}[0-9]{4}[A-Z]{1}[1-9A-Z]{1}Z[0-9A-Z]{1}$` fails, or the checksum (mod-36 algorithm over the first 14 chars, alphabet `0-9A-Z`, weights alternating 1 and 2) fails. Weight 35.
  - `TAX_MATH_MISMATCH`: stated CGST+SGST+IGST differs from taxable_value × rate by more than ₹1. Weight 30.
  - `WRONG_TAX_TYPE`: intra-state (vendor state == place of supply) but IGST charged, or inter-state but CGST/SGST charged. Weight 30.
  - `INVALID_GST_RATE`: rate not in the valid slabs. Weight 30.
  - `DUPLICATE_INVOICE`: same vendor and invoice_number already exists, or the same vendor, date and total amount. Weight 40.
  - `AMOUNT_SPIKE`: vendor z-score > 3 or amount > Q3 + 3×IQR. Weight 30.
  - `JUST_BELOW_THRESHOLD`: total between ₹47,500 and ₹49,999 (the e-way bill ₹50,000 limit) and the vendor has 2 or more such invoices. Weight 20.
  - `ROUND_AMOUNT`: exact multiple of ₹10,000 with no paise. Weight 10.
  - `NEW_VENDOR_HIGH_VALUE`: vendor has under 3 invoices and amount above ₹1,00,000. Weight 20.
  - `FUTURE_OR_STALE_DATE`: date in the future or more than 1 year old. Weight 15.
  - `HSN_RATE_MISMATCH`: HSN→rate map mismatch for a small mapping (e.g. `1006` rice 5, `8517` phones 18, `6109` t-shirts 5/12, `9983` IT services 18, `3004` medicines 12, `8703` cars 28). Weight 20.
  - `WEEKEND_BURST`: 4 or more invoices from the same vendor on the same day. Weight 10.
- **Final score** = `min(100, round(0.45 × if_score + 0.55 × min(100, sum_of_rule_weights)))`.
  If any `high`-severity rule fires, enforce a minimum score of 70.
- **Feature contributions:** per feature, compute abs(scaled value) × a small importance weight, normalize to 100%, and return the top 6 for the bar chart.
- **Explanation template:** "This invoice from {vendor} was flagged {level} risk ({score}/100) mainly because {top 2 reasons in plain words}. Compared with this vendor's usual invoices (median ₹{x}), this one is {ratio}× higher. Recommended action: {verify/correct/escalate}."

### 6.6 Seed data (`seed.py`)
Create **12 vendors** with realistic Indian names and valid-checksum GSTINs, different amount scales:
- Sri Lakshmi Traders (33, Tamil Nadu, ~₹8k avg, groceries)
- Rao Electronics (36, Telangana, ~₹45k)
- Mehta Textiles (24, Gujarat, ~₹1.2L)
- Kumar Stationers (29, Karnataka, ~₹3k)
- Patel Pharma Distributors (24, ~₹60k)
- Bharat IT Solutions (27, Maharashtra, ~₹2.5L)
- Delhi Auto Parts (07, ~₹35k)
- Annapurna Foods (09, ~₹15k)
- Singh Hardware (07, ~₹22k)
- Chennai Packaging Co (33, ~₹18k)
- Eastern Chemicals (19, ~₹90k)
- QuickServe Logistics (29, ~₹12k)

Generate **about 600 invoices over the last 12 months** (log-normal amounts around each vendor's mean, correct tax math, correct CGST/SGST vs IGST by state, slabs by category, weekday-weighted dates, unique invoice numbers like `SLT/2026/00123`). Inject about 8% anomalies of mixed types (spikes, duplicates, bad GSTIN, tax mismatch, wrong tax type, threshold-hugging, round numbers). Pre-assign a mix of statuses so the Review Queue is populated (about 15 Pending, 5 Escalated, 5 Needs Correction, a few Verified). Add 3 sample CSV files in `/data`, with a purchase register of about 40 rows, a GSTR-2B with about 6 deliberately missing and 5 mismatched amounts, and a batch invoice file of 15 rows with 4 anomalies.

### 6.7 Reconciliation logic
Match on `(vendor GSTIN, invoice_number)`. Categories: **Matched**, **Missing in GSTR-2B** (ITC at risk = the invoice tax), **Amount Mismatch** (tax differs by more than ₹1), **Extra in 2B**. Return counts, total ITC at risk, and rows.
